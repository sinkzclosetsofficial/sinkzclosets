CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

Some useful information to help you shop with confidence.

How do I browse available products?

Visit the Shop page to browse the available catalogue. You can also use the search section on this homepage.

How do I place an order?

Select a product, add it to your cart, review your selections and proceed through the available checkout process.

Which currencies are supported?

The store supports Zambian Kwacha (ZMW) and South African Rand (ZAR). Prices are maintained independently. Currency conversion is not automatic.

Which payment methods are displayed?

MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money are the displayed Zambian mobile-money options. Capitec is a South African banking option; confirm cross-border transfer availability, fees and recipient details before paying.

How can I get assistance?

Visit our About & Contact page for the available shop contact information.
