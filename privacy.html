Privacy Policy

How Sinkzclosets handles customer information.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Introduction

Sinkzclosets respects the privacy of its customers and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information may be collected, why it is collected and how it may be used.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services used, we may collect:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Telephone number and email address.
  • Delivery address and delivery instructions.
  • Products ordered, quantities and order history.
  • Messages and enquiries sent to us.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the website.

3. How We Use Information

Customer information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and customer requests.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Arrange deliveries and communicate order updates.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our products, services and website.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorised access.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information. Information may be shared where necessary with service providers, delivery partners, payment providers or competent authorities where legally required. We aim to limit such sharing to what is reasonably necessary.

5. Payments

This website's current checkout is a demonstration and does not process online payments. Do not submit card details, PINs, passwords or mobile-money security codes through the demonstration checkout. Any future payment service will be subject to its own applicable security and privacy arrangements.

6. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, order management, recordkeeping, security and applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, customers may request access to, correction of or other lawful action concerning their personal information. Requests can be made using the contact details published on our website.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage to remember shopping-cart information and improve the shopping experience. Clearing browser data may remove locally stored cart information.

10. Third-Party Services

Links to services such as social media, messaging platforms or payment providers may lead to websites governed by their own privacy policies. Sinkzclosets does not control those external policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Sinkzclosets using the official contact details published on the About & Contact page.
